# Post 3: Don't Outsource Your Podcast to an Agency

**Author:** Jason Lemkin

**Date posted:** ~June 15, 2026 (1 day ago at time of collection)

## Post Text

Everyone in the world has a podcast now.

My #1 piece of advice: If you want me or better (I'm only so good)... do NOT outsource it to an agency.

Agencies have their place. But not here.

If you want a guest for your podcast and you didn't co-found Stripe, do it yourself. Harry Stebbings still does it all yourself.

Send the best pitch. Ask for it. You will be surprised who comes on if you do.

And if your agency does it? Well, you'll get folks selling something. That's about it.

## Annotation

Directly relevant to community-led growth: podcasts are a common community-building tool for B2B SaaS founders, and Lemkin's point is that authenticity (founder-led outreach) outperforms delegated/agency-led outreach when trying to attract high-quality guests or build trust with an audience. This mirrors a theme across other sources in this research, community work resists being fully outsourced because relationship and trust-building doesn't scale through agencies the way paid acquisition does.
